# User Guide - GeoEnergy AI Platform

Welcome to the **GeoEnergy AI – Smart Renewable Energy Intelligence Platform**! This guide assists users of all roles (Planner, GIS Analyst, Project Manager, Administrator) in operating the application's key workflows.

---

## 1. Authentication & Onboarding

### Sign-In with Standard Credentials
1. Navigate to the login screen.
2. Select standard login credentials (e.g. `planner` / `planner123`).
3. Click Login to access your role-specific dashboard.

### Sign-In with Google (OAuth 2.0)
1. On the login screen, click **Sign In with Google**.
2. Authenticate through the official Google popup.
3. If it's your first login, complete your onboarding profile details (Skills, Education, Social Links, and target role) to finalize account registry.

---

## 2. Energy Planner Siting Workflow

### Creating a Coordinate Campaign
1. Open the **Projects** view and click **Create Project**.
2. Provide a Campaign name, descriptions, and technology type (Solar, Wind, or Hybrid).
3. Access the **Interactive Map** view.
4. Click on your target coordinates on the map.
5. Provide a site name, land area (Ha), and ownership type, then click **Save Site Location**.

### Run AI Feasibility Predictions
1. Open the project details, click **Run AI Predictions**.
2. The AI returns capacity factors, seasonal energy yields, CAPEX/OPEX estimates, and LCOE payback targets.
3. Click **Submit Campaign** to freeze coordinates and trigger GIS analysis assignment.

---

## 3. GIS Coordinate Validation

### Claiming review assignment
1. Log in as a **GIS Analyst** or open the **Projects** list.
2. Select the unassigned campaign in `"Submitted"` status.
3. Click **Claim Project Review** to self-assign coordinates verification.
4. Provide review comments, terrain/slope hazard analysis, and click **Approve Siting** or **Reject Siting**.

---

## 4. Project Management & Economics

### Checking off phased milestones
1. Log in as a **Project Manager**.
2. Select your assigned campaign from the dropdown.
3. Open the **Milestones** tab and check off development stages (civil, grids connection, commissioning) as they occur.
4. View the 20-year financial forecasts payback chart updating in real time.

---

## 5. Exports Feasibility Reports

1. Open the **Reports** view.
2. Select your registered project site.
3. View high-fidelity previews for site assessment, wind yields, or CAPEX recommendations.
4. Click **Export Excel (CSV)** or **Download PDF (HTML)** to save formal attachments.

# Project Objectives

## Project Title
Solar & Wind Deployment Intelligence Platform

## Objective
The Solar & Wind Deployment Intelligence Platform is an AI-powered system designed to recommend optimal locations for renewable energy projects. The platform analyzes environmental, geographic, climatic, and infrastructure-related factors to identify suitable deployment sites.

## Goals

- Recommend optimal locations for solar and wind energy projects.
- Analyze environmental and geographic datasets.
- Predict solar and wind energy generation potential.
- Evaluate project feasibility.
- Support investment and planning decisions.
- Provide interactive dashboards for planners and analysts.

## Target Users

- Renewable Energy Planners
- GIS Analysts
- Project Managers
- Government Agencies
- Renewable Energy Companies
- Investors
- Administrators

## Expected Outcome

The platform will help organizations make data-driven decisions for renewable energy deployment using GIS, Machine Learning, satellite imagery, and environmental datasets.
